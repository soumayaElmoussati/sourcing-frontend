X10ECOM — Fulfillment & Logistics Services Agreement

1. Scope of Services / نطاق الخدمات

The Company provides: Product sourcing, Customs clearance, Warehousing, Call center and order confirmation, Order Fulfillment, Shipping & Delivery, COD Management, Returns Handling, Reporting & Analytics, sending profits to the seller worldwide.

2. Call Center / مركز الاتصال

Customers are contacted up to 3 times per day for 3 days (9 attempts). If unreachable, the order is cancelled. Upsell and Cross-sell services are available.

3. Warehousing / التخزين

Storage is free as long as there are ongoing orders. Products stored in UAE warehouses can ship to all GCC countries. Inactive goods for more than 60 days may be disposed of.

4. Fulfillment & Shipping / التنفيذ والشحن

Orders are processed professionally. Shipping is calculated based on actual or volumetric weight (whichever is higher). Formula: (L × W × H) ÷ 5000. Deliveries during business days only.

5. Delivery & Returns / التوصيل والمرتجعات

Rejected deliveries count as delivery attempts. Orders are returned to the warehouse. Valid returned items may be restocked after inspection.

6. Payments & Remittance / الدفع والتحويلات

Payments are transferred weekly. Shipping, fees, and commissions are deducted before transfer. Bank transfer fees are borne by the client. A 2% fee is deducted from each withdrawal or payout.

7. International Shipping / الشحن الدولي

Prices exclude VAT, customs duties, and government fees. All such costs are borne by the client.

8. Liability / المسؤولية

The client is responsible for product legality and quality. Damage is compensated based on the declared value.

9. Fraud Protection / الحماية من الاحتيال

The company may suspend suspicious accounts or orders. Payments may be held in case of suspected fraud.

10. Confidentiality / السرية

All data and information are confidential and will not be shared with any third party.

11. Contract Duration / مدة العقد

The contract is valid for one year. It is automatically renewed unless terminated with prior notice.

12. Governing Law / القانون

This Agreement is governed by applicable laws in the operating jurisdiction.

13. Acceptance / القبول

By signing this contract and using X10ECOM services, the client agrees to all terms and conditions stated above.
